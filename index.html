How it works

From brief to published content in three steps

Instruct

You add the links to your social channels and portals, and upload photos, brochures and any material you already have. The platform processes and structures it into your Brand Brain.

Create

You generate content in a few clicks: social posts, videos and images, SEO articles, newsletters, banners and menus — using the most suitable AI model for each output.

Publish

With a single click you publish to the channels you have connected. The platform keeps a memory of everything you produce so your communication stays consistent.

TikTok integration

How HiOneOnly uses TikTok

Connecting TikTok is optional and always initiated by you. When you choose to connect your TikTok account, you are redirected to TikTok to log in and approve access. We never ask for your TikTok password, and you can disconnect at any time.

user.info.basic

Used only to show which TikTok account is connected inside the platform (your display name and avatar), so you always know where content will be published.

video.publish

Used only to publish content you create in HiOneOnly — videos and photo posts — to your own TikTok account, with the caption you choose, and only when you click publish.

We do not read your private data, browse other users' content, or post anything without your explicit action. Use of TikTok data follows TikTok's Developer Terms and our Privacy Policy. You can revoke access anytime from the platform or from your TikTok account settings.
